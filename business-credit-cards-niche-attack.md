## Build map — SPLIT across FundBiz + MHQ (revised 22 Jun — regulatory segmentation)
**Why not BBL:** BBL is the **sole-trader / new-business** site (verified: 47 files of startup/sole-trader/vernacular "loan for a new company" content). Sole-trader cards ≤£25k are the **regulated consumer-credit slice** — so keep credit cards OFF BBL. **FundBiz is framed for incorporated/limited companies** (89× "Ltd", 5× "incorporated") = the **unregulated business-credit lane** = the right home.
- **FundBiz = the MAIN business-credit-card cluster** — best-business-credit-cards-uk, company credit card, business charge card, per-issuer reviews (Capital on Tap, Amex Business, Virgin Money) + the **rewards/cashback/avios** sub-cluster (Amex Business Gold). Framed **for limited companies**. **⚠️ correct risk wording required** (see below).
- **MerchantHQ = the PAYMENTS-TARGETED cards** — retailers/hospitality, expense/spending cards, payments+cards providers (Tide, Revolut Business), card-vs-charge for card-machine cashflow, cashback-on-fees. Merchant/payments context.
- **BBL stays out of credit cards** (sole-trader/new-business loans + company formations) — but receives the **"declined for a card → business loan"** funnel via cross-link from FundBiz.
- Cross-link FundBiz ↔ MHQ ↔ BBL; shared neutral-comparison + regulatory-identifier model.

**⚠️ Risk wording to carry on every FundBiz card page** (keeps it in the unregulated/compliant lane): frame **for limited companies / incorporated businesses**; **representative APR** worked example; "**subject to status / credit check**"; missed-payment + interest-rollup warning; the neutral-introducer disclosure; affiliate-link disclosure. Avoid sole-trader-specific (≤£25k regulated) card pages, or handle that slice separately with the consumer-credit rules.
