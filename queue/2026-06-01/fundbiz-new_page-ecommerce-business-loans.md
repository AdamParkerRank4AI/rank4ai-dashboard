  Section: What Finance Suits Online Retailers?

  Merchant Cash Advance (MCA)
  The most popular product for ecommerce businesses with regular card or gateway turnover. Repayments flex with revenue, so a slow month costs less. YouLend, Liberis and Capify advance against Stripe, PayPal, Shopify Payments and Amazon Disbursements. Advance sizes typically from £5,000 to £500,000. Factor rates from 1.15.

  Stock and Inventory Finance
  Seasonal businesses buying ahead of peak periods (Q3 stock build for Q4 sales) use revolving stock finance to fund purchase orders without tying up working capital. Specialist lenders advance against confirmed purchase orders or supplier invoices.

  Asset Finance for Warehouse and Logistics
  Racking, packing equipment, forklifts, vehicles and conveyor systems can all be financed over 24 to 60 months, keeping capital free for growth. Monthly payments are fixed, so cashflow remains predictable.

  Term Loans for Marketing Investment
  Performance marketing and ROAS-driven scaling works well with a fixed-term loan. Borrow against projected revenue, repay over 6 to 36 months. Particularly suited to Meta and Google Ads spend ahead of a product launch.

  Section: Eligibility for Ecommerce Businesses

  Most ecommerce lenders want to see: 6 or more months trading, £5,000 or more monthly gateway or card turnover, UK-registered business. Amazon-only sellers may face concentration risk queries; multi-channel sellers are viewed more favourably. Gross margin transparency helps where product-level profitability is hard to read from accounts.

  Section: How FundBiz Matches Online Sellers to Lenders

  We review your trading data, gateway statements and growth trajectory, then approach the lenders most likely to approve your application at the best rate. One application, multiple lenders, no credit-score impact from comparison. Decisions typically within 24 hours.

  FAQ Section

  Q1: Can I get an ecommerce business loan with bad credit?
  Yes. MCA lenders focus on gateway turnover, not credit score. Provided you have consistent monthly card or platform income, bad credit or CCJs do not automatically disqualify you.

  Q2: How much can an ecommerce business borrow?
  MCA facilities typically run from £5,000 to £500,000 depending on monthly turnover. Term loans and asset finance can exceed £1 million for established sellers with audited accounts.

  Q3: How quickly can I get funding for my online shop?
  MCA approvals are often same-day with funds in 24 to 48 hours. Term loans and asset finance typically take 3 to 7 business days from application to drawdown.
