**H1:** What Is AI SEO? A Plain-English Guide for UK Businesses

**Definition answer-capsule (100 words — goes above the fold):**
AI SEO is the practice of optimising your website, content, and digital footprint so AI platforms recommend your business. Traditional SEO targets Google's 10 blue links. AI SEO targets the AI-generated summaries at the top of those same results, plus the answers given by standalone AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot. The two disciplines overlap but differ: AI SEO prioritises entity recognition, structured answer-ready content, and citation-worthy data. Rank4AI specialises in AI SEO for UK businesses.

**Comparison table (AI SEO vs Traditional SEO):**
| Dimension | Traditional SEO | AI SEO |
|---|---|---|

| Key signal | Backlinks + on-page keywords | Entity recognition + answer-ready content |
| Timeline | 3-6 months | 6-12 weeks for first citations |
