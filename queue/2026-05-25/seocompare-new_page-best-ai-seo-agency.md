  ## What to look for in an AI SEO agency

  An AI SEO agency is not the same as a traditional SEO agency with AI mentioned on the homepage. Look for:
  a clear articulation of how Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity differ in how they select sources;
  evidence of tracking AI citations (not just Google rankings); case studies where the client is named and the
  metric is citations, not keyword positions.

  Red flags: guaranteed AI rankings (no agency can guarantee AI citations); agencies that repackage link-building
  as "AI optimisation"; contracts requiring 12+ months before results are measurable.
