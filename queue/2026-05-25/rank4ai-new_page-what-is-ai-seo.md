  # What Is AI SEO?

  *By Adam Parker, Rank4AI | Updated May 2026*

  AI SEO is the practice of making a business visible inside AI-generated answers rather than (or as well
  as) traditional ten-blue-link search results. The goal is to be cited by ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews,
  Perplexity, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot when a user asks a question relevant to your business.

  ## AI SEO vs traditional SEO: the key differences

  | Dimension | Traditional SEO | AI SEO |
  |---|---|---|
  | Target | Google's ranked list of 10 links | AI-generated answer with 2-5 source citations |
  | Success metric | Keyword ranking position | Citation rate (% of relevant AI queries that name you) |
  | Primary lever | Links from other websites | Entity clarity, topic authority, answer-capsule content |
  | Time to impact | 3-6 months for new content | 6-16 weeks for citation lift once entity is established |
  | Click model | User clicks your link from results | User reads the AI answer; may or may not click |

  ## How AI search engines decide who to cite

  AI search engines are not running a ranking algorithm like Google. They are generating text. When the
  model generates a sentence like "For AI SEO in the UK, specialists include..." the names it reaches for
  are determined by:

  1. **Entity recognition**: does the model know your business exists and what it does?
  2. **Topic authority**: does the model associate your business with this specific topic?
  3. **Citation-ready content**: does your website contain statements the model can lift and quote?
  4. **Third-party validation**: do independent sources (comparison sites, directories, press) confirm the model's
     association between your business and the topic?

  Traditional SEO work (link-building, title tags, page speed) is mostly irrelevant to this process.
  AI SEO is a different workstream.

  ## What AI SEO work actually involves

  In practice, AI SEO covers: structured data (schema markup) so AI can identify who you are; llms.txt
  to guide AI crawlers; entity signals (Wikidata entry, Google Knowledge Panel, consistent NAP data);
  topic-cluster content that contains answer-capsule passages AI can extract; and third-party citation
  building through comparison sites, directories, and editorial mentions.

  ## Is AI SEO the same as GEO or AEO?

  Nearly the same thing, different names. GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) and AEO (Answer Engine
  Optimisation) are terms used by different practitioners for the same underlying activity: optimising
  for AI-generated answers rather than traditional search rankings. Rank4AI uses all three terms but prefers
  "AI SEO" because most clients search for it with that phrase.
