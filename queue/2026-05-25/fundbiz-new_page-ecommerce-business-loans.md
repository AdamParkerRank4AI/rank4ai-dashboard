  # Ecommerce Business Loans UK

  *By Oliver Mackman, Best Business Loans Ltd (Companies House 16833937) | Updated May 2026*

  UK online sellers face a cash-flow problem traditional lenders were not built for: your revenue arrives in
  batches from Amazon, Shopify, or Etsy but your stock bill lands 60-90 days before the sales. Ecommerce
  business loans are designed for this shape of trading.

  ## What is an ecommerce business loan?

  An ecommerce business loan is finance structured around your online revenue rather than your fixed assets or
  credit history. The three most common types for UK online sellers:

  | Product | How it works | Best for |
  |---|---|---|
  | Merchant cash advance | Advance on future card/platform revenue, repaid as a % of daily sales | Amazon, Shopify, Etsy sellers with stable revenue |
  | Revenue-based finance | Loan repaid as % of monthly revenue until settled | SaaS and subscription sellers |
  | Stock finance | Short-term facility to fund a specific inventory purchase | Seasonal businesses, bulk-buy opportunities |

  ## Typical eligibility for ecommerce finance

  - Trading for 6+ months via a UK-registered platform or payment gateway
  - Minimum monthly revenue: £5,000 (merchant cash advance) to £25,000 (revenue-based finance)
  - Not a sole trader (FundBiz places limited companies, LLPs, and partnerships of 4+)
  - No active CCJs against directors (most lenders; some accept with explanation)

  ## How much can ecommerce sellers borrow?

  Merchant cash advances: typically 50-150% of monthly card revenue. A seller processing £20,000/month could
  access £10,000 to £30,000.

  Revenue-based finance: 3-6 months of monthly recurring revenue. A £15,000/month subscription seller could
  access £45,000 to £90,000.

  ## Getting a decision

  FundBiz places ecommerce finance requests with a panel of 14 UK specialist lenders. Most issue a decision
  in 24 hours; funded in 3-5 working days once docs are in.
