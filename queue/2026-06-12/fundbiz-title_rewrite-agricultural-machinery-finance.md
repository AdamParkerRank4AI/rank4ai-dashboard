## Title rewrite

**Page:** /asset-finance/tractor-finance/

```
Tractor and agricultural machinery finance UK
```

### Proposed title
```
Agricultural Machinery Finance UK: Tractors, Combines & Farm Kit
```

### Meta description (currently auto-generated from asset.intro first 155 chars)
Auto-generated intro starts: "Tractor and agricultural machinery finance funds tractors, combines, sprayers, balers..." — this is descriptive but doesn't front-load the query term in the meta. Consider a dedicated description field:

```
Agricultural machinery finance for UK farms. Tractors, combines, sprayers and telehandlers funded on hire purchase or sale-and-leaseback. Repayments shaped around farm income cycles.
```

### Implementation note
The title is generated by `src/pages/asset-finance/[slug].astro` as `${asset.name} UK`. To change the title for the tractor-finance slug without affecting all other asset types, add an optional `titleOverride` field to the `AssetType` type in `asset-types.ts` and update `[slug].astro` to use it when present:

```typescript
// In asset-types.ts (AssetType interface)
titleOverride?: string;

// In tractor-finance entry
titleOverride: 'Agricultural Machinery Finance UK: Tractors, Combines & Farm Kit',
```

Or rename `asset.name` to 'Agricultural machinery finance' (drop 'Tractor and') — this changes H1 and breadcrumb too, which may be better for consistency.

### Rationale
1. 'Agricultural machinery finance' is the primary query (pos 12, imp 15). 'Tractor' appears at position 4 in the current title — after three weaker words. Front-loading the exact query is the highest-leverage change.
2. 'Combines & Farm Kit' in the subtitle signals breadth (not just tractors) and improves click relevance for searches about specific machines other than tractors.
3. 'UK' is retained for geo-relevance.
4. 15 impressions is low but at pos 12 this is close to page 1 — a title fix has a reasonable chance of pulling it into the top 10 where impression volume rises sharply.
