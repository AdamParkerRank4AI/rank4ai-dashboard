  H2: How ChatGPT Decides What to Recommend

  ChatGPT's search-enabled responses draw from Bing's web index, not Google's. An agency that focuses only on Google is likely missing the Bing signals that determine ChatGPT citation. The five signals most associated with ChatGPT citation in SEOCompare's 2026 testing:

  1. Bing index presence and freshness. Pages not indexed by Bing within the last 90 days have low citation rates even with strong Google positions.

  2. Structured data (schema.org). ChatGPT's model shows consistent preference for pages with Organisation, Article, and FAQPage schema that matches the visible content.

  3. Entity consistency across sources. Businesses described consistently across Wikipedia, Wikidata, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile, and Bing Places are cited more often than those with inconsistent data.

  4. Answer-ready paragraphs. ChatGPT extracts verbatim passages. Pages with a direct 40-50 word answer in the first paragraph, followed by supporting evidence, are cited more than pages with buried or discursive answers.

  5. External citation from authoritative UK sources. ChatGPT's citations bias toward content that has been referenced by established media, trade bodies, or databases.

  ---

  H2: What to Look for in a ChatGPT SEO Agency

  Not all "AI SEO agencies" do the same work. Before briefing an agency, ask these four questions.

  Do they track Bing rankings separately from Google? If the answer is no, they are not doing ChatGPT SEO; they are doing Google SEO and calling it AI SEO.

  Can they show you citation testing methodology? A credible agency will run before-and-after citation tests across a defined query set. They should show you what they tested, how they tested it, and what changed.

  Do they build entity data separately from link building? These are different work streams with different suppliers and different timelines. Conflating them is a red flag.

  Do they have named, verifiable case studies from UK clients? Anonymous case studies are not evidence.
