  H2: AI SEO vs Traditional SEO: What Is Different?

  Traditional SEO optimises pages to rank in a list of blue links. AI SEO optimises an entity, a business or person, to be cited as an answer by AI systems that do not always show those links.

  The key differences:

  Traditional SEO targets keyword rankings. AI SEO targets citation presence.

  Traditional SEO focuses on page authority (backlinks, content depth). AI SEO focuses on entity trust signals (consistent structured data, named authorship, cross-platform citations, answer-ready content structure).

  Traditional SEO is measured in rank positions and organic traffic. AI SEO is measured in AI citation rate: how often does your business appear as a named answer inside ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews when your target queries are asked.

  The overlap is real: strong traditional SEO is a prerequisite for AI visibility, because around 70% of AI Overview citations draw from page-one organic results. But strong SEO alone does not guarantee AI citation. The additional layer is what separates AI SEO as a discipline.

  ---

  H2: How AI Systems Decide What to Cite

  AI systems do not rank pages. They identify entities, businesses, people, products, and organisations, and assess their trust signals. For a UK business, that means:

  Structured data on the website (schema.org Organisation, Person, FAQPage, HowTo, Article types). AI systems parse schema to understand what the entity is, who runs it, and what it does.

  Consistent entity mentions across the open web. ChatGPT uses Bing's index. If a business is described consistently as the same thing across 40-plus external sources, the AI assigns it higher authority for that topic.

  Named authorship. E-E-A-T signals (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) carry into AI citation. Anonymised or committee-authored content is consistently cited less often than content with a named, verifiable expert author.

  Answer-ready content structure. AI systems extract short, direct answers from long documents. Pages structured with a concise answer in the first 40-50 words, followed by supporting evidence, are cited more often than pages that bury the answer in the third paragraph.

  ---

  H2: What AI SEO Involves in Practice

  An AI SEO engagement typically covers five signal areas.

  Entity building: establishing a consistent identity for the business across the website, Google Business Profile, Wikidata, Wikipedia, and 30-plus data aggregators AI systems index.

  Content restructuring: reformatting existing pages with answer capsules, named authorship, and schema markup. No rewriting required for well-established content.

  Citation building: building structured presence on the specific directories, databases, and publisher sites that ChatGPT's Bing index, Gemini's training data, and Google's Knowledge Graph draw from.

  Competitor gap analysis: identifying which AI answers your competitors appear in that you do not, and prioritising the work to close those gaps.

  Measurement: tracking AI citation rate across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot, and Google AI Overviews on a monthly basis.

  ---

  H2: How Long Does AI SEO Take to Work?

  Entity and citation work produces measurable citation improvements within 60 to 90 days for most UK businesses. Content restructuring can work within a single crawl cycle. Full coverage across all major AI systems is typically achieved at four to six months.
